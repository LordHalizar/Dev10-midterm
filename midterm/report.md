# IDB Analysis of Population Data
Using the dataset provided by the International Database, I will be analyzing population trends and estimates. This will be done to identify key patterns in population composition and changes over time

## Dataset
From the IDB dataset provided, I will be focusing on total population, age-ranges, median age, & yearly data from ten sample countries. Of the countries selected, two were chosen for their high population values, two were chosen for the known slower (even negative) population growth, in addition with a number of countries from Northern America and Europe for comparison.

## Initial Hypothesis
The United States of America has an aging population and life expectancy is increasing.
